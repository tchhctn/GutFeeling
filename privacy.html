Last updated: [DATE]

Who can use GutFeeling

GutFeeling is for people aged 18 and over. Accounts for anyone under 18 are not allowed and will be removed.

What we collect

  • Your email address and login details (or your Google account email if you sign in with Google).
  • Your profile: first name, date of birth, gender, city, photo, bio, favourite safe spot.
  • Your coeliac or gluten-free status and kitchen status. This is health-related information, so we treat it with extra care and only show it on your profile to other signed-in users.
  • Your likes, passes, matches, messages, blocks and reports.

Who sees what

  • Other signed-in members can see your profile: name, age (not your birth date), city, photo, bio, status and kitchen.
  • Only people you match with can message you.
  • Photos are stored at a link that is not listed publicly, but anyone who has the link can open it.
  • Photos are re-saved when uploaded, which removes hidden location data (EXIF) from them.

How your data is stored

Data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase. We do not sell your data or show you ads.

Your choices

  • Hide your profile any time from the account menu.
  • Block or report anyone from their card or chat.
  • Delete your account from the account menu. This removes your profile, photo, matches and messages.
